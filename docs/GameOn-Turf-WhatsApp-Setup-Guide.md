# Get Your Turf on WhatsApp Booking

**GameOn · WhatsApp Booking** — a one-time Meta setup guide for turf owners.

This sets up your business on Meta and connects your WhatsApp number to the GameOn booking bot. You do it **once** per turf. Pick **Guide 1** if GameOn sent you a “Connect WhatsApp” link — it's the 5-minute route. Use **Guide 2** only if you're setting everything up by hand.
# **Before you start — have these ready**
- A **personal Facebook account** you control (you'll be the admin behind the business).
- Your **business name** exactly as you want it shown to customers.
- A **business email** address Meta can contact you on.
- The **turf's mobile number** that will be used for WhatsApp.
- **Proof of the business** for verification — any ONE showing your legal name + address: GST certificate, Shop & Establishment / trade licence, incorporation certificate, or a recent utility bill / bank statement in the business name.
- Your **business address, phone, and website or social page**.

**Do NOT delete WhatsApp from your turf number**
If your turf already uses this number on the WhatsApp Business app, **keep it.** GameOn connects using **Coexistence** (the app and the booking bot share one number). Removing the number to “migrate” locks you out of the app permanently. Just make sure a **profile picture is set on the number before you connect it.**

**GUIDE 1  ·  THE FAST WAY  (≈ 5 MINUTES)**
**Connect with GameOn's link**

GameOn sends you a **“Connect WhatsApp”** link (this is Meta's official Embedded Signup). One guided popup creates your business portfolio, sets up your WhatsApp account, adds and verifies your number, and connects GameOn automatically — no separate verification paperwork to chase up front.
- Open the **Connect WhatsApp** link GameOn sends you, on a phone or computer.
- Log in with your **personal Facebook account** when the popup asks.
- Choose **Create a new business portfolio** (or pick an existing one), and confirm your business name and email.
- Enter your **turf's mobile number**, set the **display name** (the real turf name — WhatsApp reviews it) and category (Sports/Recreation).
- If the number is already on the WhatsApp Business app, pick **Coexistence** when offered so you keep the app.
- Verify the number with the **OTP code** Meta sends by SMS or voice call.
- Finish the popup. GameOn is now connected as your provider — **that's it.**

**GUIDE 2  ·  THE MANUAL WAY**
**Set up & verify your portfolio yourself**

Use this only if you don't have a Connect link. Budget **30–45 minutes**, plus review time from Meta. Do it on a computer.
## **Part A — Create your Meta Business Portfolio**
- Go to **business.facebook.com** and log in with your personal Facebook account.
- Click **Create a business portfolio** (older name: “Business Manager”). If you have none yet, you're prompted automatically.
- Fill in your **business name**, **your name** (the admin) and **business email**.
- Click **Create**, then open Meta's confirmation email and click the link to **confirm your email.**

## **Part B — Verify the business**
This proves you're a real business, raises your messaging limits and unlocks full features.
- In your portfolio open **Business Settings** (gear icon) → **Security Centre** (or **Business Info → Verification**).
- Click **Start verification** and enter your legal business details — name, address, phone, website.
- Enter them **exactly as they appear on your documents**. Mismatches are the #1 reason verification is rejected.
- If Meta doesn't find you automatically, **upload a document** (GST certificate, trade licence, utility bill or bank statement) showing the same legal name + address.
- Verify the business phone or email with the code Meta sends, then **Submit**. Review usually takes minutes to a few days (occasionally up to ~2 weeks).

**You can keep going while this is pending**
Business Verification can run in the background. A blue badge is separate — that's the optional paid **Meta Verified** subscription, not required for bookings.
## **Part C — Add & verify the turf's WhatsApp number**
- Go to **WhatsApp Manager** (business.facebook.com/wa/manage) inside your portfolio.
- Choose **Add phone number** to create a new WhatsApp Business Account.
- Set the **display name** (the real turf name — WhatsApp reviews it), **category** (Sports/Recreation) and language.
- If the number is already on the WhatsApp Business app, choose **Coexistence** so you keep the app.
- Verify the number with the **OTP** sent by SMS or voice call.
- Add GameOn as a partner: **Business Settings → Partners → Add partner**, and share your WhatsApp account with the **Partner Business ID** GameOn gives you.

**Number limits to plan around**
One number per WhatsApp account, and a portfolio can hold a **maximum of 4 Coexistence numbers.** Several turfs on one portfolio is fine; a very large operator may need more than one portfolio.
## **Part D — Hand off to GameOn**
Once the number shows **Connected** in WhatsApp Manager, send GameOn:
- Your **Phone Number ID** (WhatsApp Manager → your number — the numeric ID, not the phone number itself).
- Your **WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID**.
- Confirmation you've **added them as a partner**.
GameOn then wires up the booking Flow on their side — nothing more for you to touch.
# If you get stuck

| What you see | What it means / how to fix it |
|---|---|
| Confirmation email never arrives | Check your spam folder, then resend from Business Settings → Business Info. |
| Verification was rejected | Your legal name or address didn't match the document. Re-enter them to match the paperwork exactly. |
| "This number is already registered" | It's on the WhatsApp app already. Choose Coexistence — do NOT delete it from the app. |
| The OTP code never arrives | Switch between SMS and voice call, and confirm the number can receive messages/calls right now. |
| GameOn can't see your account | You haven't shared it as a Partner yet. Do the "Add partner" step and re-send them the IDs. |
