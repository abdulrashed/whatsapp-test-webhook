# Meta Business Setup Guide

A step-by-step guide to create a Meta (Facebook) Business account, set up a Business
Portfolio, verify the business, and give access to **rahmanvapu@gmail.com**.

> You need a personal Facebook account to sign in with. Meta Business tools are managed
> through that login — you don't create a separate "business login".

---

## Step 1 — Create your Meta Business Suite / Business Portfolio

1. Go to **https://business.facebook.com/**
2. Log in with your personal Facebook account (or create one first at
   https://www.facebook.com/).
3. Click **Create account** (top right) / **Create a business portfolio**.
4. Enter:
   - **Business/Portfolio name** (this is public — use your real business name)
   - **Your name**
   - **Business email** (use a business address you can access for verification emails)
5. Click **Submit / Create**.
6. Check that email inbox and click the **confirmation link** Meta sends.

You now have a **Business Portfolio** (Meta's newer name for a Business Manager / Business
Account). All settings live in **Business Settings**:
**https://business.facebook.com/settings**

---

## Step 2 — Fill in your business details

Before verification, complete your business info so it matches official records.

1. Open **Business Settings** → **Business Info** (left menu).
   - Direct link: **https://business.facebook.com/settings/info**
2. Fill in:
   - Legal business name (exactly as on your registration / tax documents)
   - Registered address
   - Phone number
   - Website
3. Save. Consistency here matters — verification checks these against public/official records.

---

## Step 3 — Verify your business

Business verification is done in the **Security Center**.

1. Go to **Business Settings** → **Security Center**.
   - Direct link: **https://business.facebook.com/settings/security**
2. Find **Business verification** and click **Start verification**.
3. Provide the required details:
   - Legal business name, address, phone number, website
4. **Confirm the business** — Meta matches your details against official/public sources.
5. **Verify your connection to the business** — Meta sends a code by one of:
   - Phone call / SMS to the business number
   - Email to a domain-matched business email
   - Enter the code to confirm.
6. **Upload documents if requested** (only if Meta can't auto-verify):
   - Business registration / incorporation certificate
   - Tax document, utility bill, or bank statement showing the business name & address
7. Submit. Review usually takes **a few minutes to a few business days**.

> Tip: The name, address and phone on your documents must match what you entered in
> Step 2 exactly, or verification will be rejected.

---

## Step 4 — Give access to rahmanvapu@gmail.com

The person you invite must have a **Facebook account that uses rahmanvapu@gmail.com**.
If that account doesn't exist yet, create it first at https://www.facebook.com/.

1. Go to **Business Settings** → **Users** → **People**.
   - Direct link: **https://business.facebook.com/settings/people**
2. Click **Add / Invite people**.
3. Enter the email: **rahmanvapu@gmail.com**
4. Choose the access level:
   - **Full control / Admin** — can manage everything (choose this if they should manage
     the whole business), **or**
   - **Partial access / Employee** — limited to assigned assets only.
5. (Optional) On the next screen, assign specific **assets** (Pages, Ad accounts,
   WhatsApp account, etc.) and toggle the permissions for each.
6. Click **Invite / Send request**.
7. The person receives an **email invitation** at rahmanvapu@gmail.com — they must
   **open it and click Accept** to gain access.

> If the invite email doesn't arrive, ask them to check spam, or resend from
> **Settings → Users → People** (the pending invite shows there).

## Notes for the GameOn WhatsApp integration

- Business verification is **required** before you can move a WhatsApp Business API number
  out of the limited/unverified tier and raise messaging limits.
- The **WhatsApp Account** appears as an asset under Business Settings once your WhatsApp
  Business Platform / app is connected — you can assign it to users in Step 4.
